North Island — your route
⛴
Cook Strait Ferry — Wellington → Picton
Interislander or Bluebridge · ~3h20–3h45 + 45–60 min check-in
Book vehicle space early Southerlies cancel sailings Campervan ~NZ$270–400
Late Dec–Jan is the busiest time of year on the Strait — book the van 4–8+ weeks out (campervans are priced by length). Big southerlies cancel sailings, so keep the day after the crossing loose. Confirm with your rental company whether the van goes on the ferry or you swap vehicles at the terminals. The last hour through Queen Charlotte Sound is the best part — be on deck.

SH35
Pacific Coast Highway — Ōpōtiki → East Cape → Gisborne
~330 km of slow coastal road · allow 2 days
2026 storm damage Single-lane sections, delays Sparse fuel & shops
Badly damaged in early 2026 storms. The Pōtaka–Te Araroa section reopened in Feb 2026 for 7am–7pm only, with five single-lane signal sections, reduced speeds and ~45 min delays, and NZTA described it as fragile and liable to close suddenly. Check the latest bulletin before committing to the East Cape loop. Fill up in Ōpōtiki or Gisborne. The East Cape lighthouse road is ~20 km of gravel.

SH38 Te Urewera — Murupara ↔ Waikaremoana ↔ Wairoa
Rotorua → Lake Waikaremoana ~3.5h · Waikaremoana → Wairoa ~1h15
~27 km still unsealed Check rental terms No fuel in Te Urewera
Slow, narrow and winding through deep forest. The last reported unsealed stretch is ~27 km between Aniwaniwa and Mangapapa bridges (on the lake side). Some rental agreements exclude gravel state highways — check yours. Fill up in Murupara (west) or Wairoa (east). Whirinaki’s access road via Minginui is also gravel.

SH25
Coromandel Peninsula — SH25 / SH25A / 309 Rd
Thames → Coromandel Town → Hahei · twisty coast roads
Slips after heavy rain Gravel north of Colville Holiday queues
Storms keep hitting the peninsula (major closures in 2023 and again in Jan 2026); roads and DOC tracks (Cathedral Cove included) close after heavy rain. Beyond Colville the road to Fletcher/Stony Bay is gravel and often excluded from rental insurance. Expect queues around Thames and Hahei between Christmas and mid-January.

SH1
Desert Road & Tongariro — SH1 / SH47 / SH48
Tūrangi → Waiouru · Whakapapa / National Park Village
No Crossing parking at Mangatepopo No fuel Tūrangi–Waiouru Snow closures rare in summer
Exposed ~1,000m plateau; summer closures are usually crashes or high wind rather than snow. There is no summer parking for Crossing walkers at Mangatepopo — use a shuttle. The 2025 fire burnt the area around the Mangatepopo road; respect closures and the rāhui.

SH3
Awakino Gorge / Mt Messenger — Waitomo ↔ New Plymouth
~2.5h · or SH43 Forgotten World Highway to Taumarunui
Bypass under construction Slips in the gorge SH43 now fully sealed
The only main road into Taranaki from the north. The Mt Messenger bypass (Te Ara o Te Ata) was still being built in 2026, so expect stop/go works. SH43 (Forgotten World Highway) is now fully sealed through the Tāngarākau Gorge but is narrow, winding and has no fuel for long stretches — fill up in Stratford or Taumarunui.

SH1
Northland — Brynderwyns, SH12 kauri coast, Far North
Auckland → Whangārei ~2h · Paihia → Cape Reinga ~3h
Ninety Mile Beach: not for rentals Brynderwyns open over summer SH12 narrow & twisty
The SH1 Brynderwyn Hills had long repair closures in 2024 and weekend closures in Nov 2025, timed to avoid summer — check before you go. Rental agreements prohibit driving on Ninety Mile Beach or down Te Paki Stream. SH12 through Waipoua Forest is slow with lots of tight bends.

🎄
Holiday traffic — Christmas & New Year
Peak outbound ~24–27 Dec · peak return ~2–5 Jan
Avoid leaving Auckland 26–27 Dec Use NZTA holiday predictor
The big city exits (SH1 north and south of Auckland, SH2/SH25 to the Coromandel and Tauranga, SH1 north of Wellington) jam on the days around Christmas and when everyone heads home in early January. Shift transit days earlier or later, or drive early morning. NZTA publishes a holiday traffic prediction map.

South Island — north-west & West Coast
SH60
Tākaka Hill — the only road into Golden Bay
Motueka → Tākaka · ~1h of hairpins
Slips close it Occasional night maintenance closures
A big, winding hill climb and the only road access to Golden Bay; slips have closed it for long periods before. If it closes, Golden Bay is cut off by road. Check before you commit to Heaphy logistics.

SH6/67
Buller Gorge, Karamea Highway & the Punakaiki coast
Murchison → Westport ~1h20 · Westport → Karamea ~1h30 (dead end)
Rockfall & slips in heavy rain Ōpārara road: gravel Karamea = dead end
The Buller Gorge (one-lane Hawks Crag) and the Westport–Greymouth coast road via Punakaiki both close for rockfall and slips after West Coast downpours. SH67 to Karamea is the only way in and out. The road to the Ōpārara arches is ~16 km of gravel, and Kōhaihai (Heaphy end) is 15 min past Karamea.

SH7
Lewis Pass — West Coast to Hanmer / Christchurch
Reefton → Springs Junction → Hanmer turn-off · alternative to Arthur’s Pass
Lower, gentler than Arthur’s Pass Long gaps between fuel
Usually straightforward in summer. Good finish to the trip via Maruia Hot Springs and Hanmer if you want to skip Arthur’s Pass. Fuel up in Reefton or Springs Junction.

South Island — other roads (from the March trip)
SH94
Homer Tunnel — Te Anau to Milford
Te Anau → Milford Sound · 2.5h
Avalanche risk lower in summer Tunnel closures: no notice Rockfall on approach No fuel Milford side
The tunnel closes without warning for avalanche control blasting — typically 1–3h wait. Summer avalanche risk is low, but rockfall closures still happen. Check status before departing Te Anau. Fill up in Te Anau (no fuel at Milford). No cell coverage for most of the road.

SH6
Haast Pass — Wānaka to West Coast
Wānaka → Haast → Franz Josef · 3.8h
Floods & slips: closes overnight Single-lane sections No fuel 145km stretch Check before 7am departure
Most likely closure on your whole route. Heavy rain (common year-round on the Coast) causes slips and washouts — can close for hours or days. Fill tank completely in Wānaka. Carry water and food. Blue Pools / Thunder Creek Falls make good stops but add time — don't push into darkness.

SH80
SH80 — Lake Pukaki to Aoraki / Mt Cook
Mackenzie → Mt Cook Village · 55km dead-end
Dead-end — same road back Flash flooding in gorge Closes in severe weather
SH80 runs 55km up the Tasman Valley — dead-end, same road in and out. Closes in severe weather or flooding (happened several times in 2024/25). Check status before committing to the drive. No fuel at Mt Cook Village — fill at Twizel or Tekapo.

SH73
Arthur's Pass — West Coast to CHC
Greymouth / Hokitika → Christchurch · 2.5h from pass
Snow & ice: May require chains Rockfall in gorge sections Summer: usually clear
Dec–Jan is generally fine, though a cold southerly can still bring snow above 900m. The Otira Gorge section is narrow and twisting. Bealey Bridge section occasionally closes for repairs. If coming from the West Coast, check conditions at Greymouth i-SITE before heading over.

SH6
SH6 — Bluff / Stewart Island return to Haast
Bluff → Queenstown → Wānaka → Haast · 6.5h
Very long drive day SH6 Queenstown section: busy Fill up in Queenstown
6.5h is a big day. Consider splitting overnight in Wānaka. The stretch from Te Anau to Queenstown (SH6) is well-maintained but busy with campervans. Queenstown to Wānaka has good passing opportunities.
